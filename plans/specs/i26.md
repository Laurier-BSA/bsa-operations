# i26 spec: Features desk, pitch-to-publish pipeline + Alumni role + handbook

**Goal.** One pipeline in Content Directory takes every alumni profile, world news explainer and advocacy week post from pitch to published, with two sources and a second-exec check enforced before anything goes out, and every profile ending in an invite to a (virtual) speaker meet.

**Design choice.** No new database. Content Directory already relates to Initiatives (`Initiative` ↔ `Content`) and holds the publish date and production `Status`. The desk adds a gate in front of that: its own `Desk stage` select, pitch and check fields, and a `Desk check` formula that says what is missing. The profiled person or speaker lives in Contacts (external people, decided in bsa-system §11). A speaker meet is an Initiatives row, linked through the existing `Initiative` relation, so no new Initiatives property. The Alumni role already exists; the desk is shown with a new `Delegation` option, so no `Role` filter changes.

Schema checked 25 Sep 2026. Facts that shaped this:
- Content Directory (`collection://206ac90c-76f1-80f1-9a34-000bc32c4222`, inline on Home / Marketing) has only: Content name (title), Content type (select: Reels, Social/ Interactive Posts, Informative Posts, Poster, Newsletter, Booth), Film date, Publish date, Initiative (relation), Item (select: Marketing, Creative), Owner (person), Platform (multi-select), Status (status: Not started, Drafting, Filming, Editing, Scheduled, Published). Templates: Reel, Graphic. Views: By Content, By Status, All Content, Calendar, By Initiative. The M1 fields in `08-marketing.md` are not built yet; nothing here collides with them.
- `Status` is a status property. The DDL tool has no syntax for status options, so the desk stages go in a separate select. `Status` stays Marketing's production state.
- `Owner` is a person field. An alumna may not be a workspace member, so the writer is a relation to Team Directory.
- Team Directory `Role` already has `Alumni` (gray). Mushfiqa is tagged `Events` + `Alumni`. None of the four Team Directory views filters on Role. i30 filters its credit view on Role not containing `Alumni`, which is correct: the alumna is not in the socials credit race.
- Contacts (`collection://19aac90c-76f1-801c-a70e-000b6bb823d7`) has `Type` (multi-select: Sponsor, Club, Vendor, Venue, SU Staff, Performer, Service Provider, Fundraising) and no field for who a person represents, which the SU asks for on speakers.
- SU Guides already has `BSA: Awareness posts on world news` (Policy, Marketing + Creative, marked "Proposed by Claude... Confirm or edit") and `BSA: 2026-27 direction: light on facilities`. It conflicts with `BSA: Marketing posts without a second approval`; that row gets a cross-reference (section 5).
- Media Directory (`collection://2ebac90c-76f1-8027-aca6-000b6c806d76`) is Name, URL, Initiative only. Not touched: profile photos stay in the piece's page body until M1 adds `Asset`.
- Brainstorm ideas feeding this: `Features series: South Asian voices in Canada` (3e6ac90c76f1814187e4f3f7bf0cb721), `World news awareness series` (3e6ac90c76f18151b004c9d57a15c7ec), `Campus advocacy week` (3e6ac90c76f1817d9749d9c2ea716482), `Virtual speaker meet: a Bangladeshi or South Asian leader` (3e6ac90c76f181df8cb2f1d6d79cf0fe), `Speaker night` (3e6ac90c76f18130b7b3c5e3f3c24e11). All `Submitted`. Not changed by this build; the VP meeting decides them.
- Existing handbook format (Alumni Handbook): callout line at top, `##` sections, short bullets, one numbered list. The new handbook copies that.

## 6. Handbook copy: Features Desk Handbook

Exact page content:

---

> 🗞️ **For whoever writes for BSA's Features desk: alumni, Marketing, and any exec writing a world news or advocacy post.** Read it once. After that, the checklist at the bottom is all you need.

## What the desk does

- **Features: South Asian voices in Canada.** One profile a month. A Bangladeshi or South Asian person in Canada doing something worth hearing about. Every profile ends with an invite to a BSA speaker meet, usually online.
- **World news.** Short explainers on news that matters to Bangladeshi and South Asian students. What happened, why it matters, where to learn more, how to help.
- **Advocacy week.** One week on one cause. A post each day, plus one Concourse table.

The Features editor is an alumna on the desk. The Marketing VP approves pitches and posts. Everything lives in Content Directory, on the Desk pipeline board below.

## From pitch to published

1. **Pitch.** New row in Content Directory with the `Desk piece` template. Pick a `Series`. Fill `Pitch` in two lines: who or what, and why now. Add yourself as `Writer`. Stage: `1 Pitched`.
2. **Approve.** The Marketing VP looks at pitches every Monday. Yes: stage `2 Approved` and a `Publish date`. No: `Declined` or `Parked`, with the reason in `Desk notes`.
3. **Draft.** Stage `3 Drafting`. Write in the row's page. Find two sources (see below) and paste them into `Source 1` and `Source 2`.
   - Profiles: add the person to Contacts (Type `Featured`, and `Speaker` if they may speak) and link them in `Featured person`. Interviews can be a video call. Send them the quotes and photo you plan to use. Tick `Consent` only when they say yes in writing.
4. **Check.** Stage `4 Check`, at least 5 days before the publish date. Ask a second exec to check it. Not you, and not the person who pitched it with you. They read it against both sources, fix the tone, then add themselves to `Checked by`. Aim for 48 hours.
5. **Cleared.** When `Desk check` says **Clear to publish**, set stage `5 Cleared`. The Marketing VP schedules it (`Status` Scheduled) and posts it. Writers never post from the BSA account.
6. **After.** For profiles, ask the person to speak and set `Invite to speak`. If they say yes, tell the Events VP the same day.

If `Desk check` says "Needs: ...", it lists exactly what is missing. Fix those, nothing else.

## Sources that count

- Two sources, from two different organizations, for every piece.
- Good: established news outlets (Reuters, AP, BBC, CBC, The Daily Star, Prothom Alo, Dawn), UN agencies, government sites, university research, the person's own employer or organization page.
- Not enough on its own: social media posts, screenshots, forwarded messages, Wikipedia, a single opinion piece.
- Profiles: the person's official page plus one other public page about them. Your interview is extra, not a source.
- Write down the date you checked. News moves.

## Rules for world news and advocacy posts

- Explain. Do not attack. Criticise actions and policies, never a people, faith, ethnicity or nationality.
- Nothing hateful, nothing that mocks, nothing that singles out a group.
- Keep facts and opinion apart. If it is our view, say "we think".
- No graphic images. Put a content warning on the first slide for violence, disaster or death.
- If a story may hit members personally, add where to get support: the Laurier Student Wellness Centre.
- "How to help" means learning more, attending, or giving to a registered charity through its own website. BSA never collects money itself. Any BSA fundraiser runs through the SU and is set up at least 21 days ahead. Set `Money ask` to `Waiting on SU` until the SU says yes.
- Got something wrong? Fix the caption within 24 hours and say what changed in the caption. Log it in `Desk notes`.
- Hateful comments: hide them. A conduct or wellbeing concern about a member goes to the SU Reporting Form, not into Notion.

## Posters on campus (Waterloo)

- The SU event request must be approved first.
- Club logo and the SU or Clubs and Associations logo on it.
- SU desk approves it, then the Dean of Students office across the hall stamps it. Then tick `Stamped`.
- Bulletin boards and designated cement walls only. Never glass, painted drywall, Concourse pillars, stairwells or the library.
- 25 posters per event, at most. Take them all down within 24 hours after the event.

## Speaker meets

- A profile's invite becomes a speaker meet run by the Events team, usually online.
- Online events still need the SU event form, 21 days ahead.
- The form names every speaker and whether they speak for themselves or an organization. Put that in their Contacts row under `Represents`.
- One speaker keeps it a standard event. Two or more makes it Large: 8 weeks' notice.
- Speakers volunteer. No payment or gift without Finance approving it first. If they want their company promoted, that is a sponsorship and needs the SU sponsorship form. Nobody at BSA signs anything; the SU does.
- Link the profile row's `Initiative` to the speaker meet so both show together.

## Before you set 4 Check

- [ ] Series and Pitch filled
- [ ] Two sources linked
- [ ] Profiles: person in Contacts, Consent ticked
- [ ] Money ask set
- [ ] Poster: Stamped
- [ ] Caption under 150 words, one call to action
