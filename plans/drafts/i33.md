# Winter 2027 roadmap, per team

Draft for item #i33. Term runs roughly Jan to Apr 2027. New direction from Safdar (25 Sep 2026): fewer events that need heavy coordination with university facilities. Favour virtual workshops and speaker meets, picnics, team socials and sport, campus advocacy, and media posts on world news. Boshontoboron (Sat 6 Feb 2027, Farhan's initiative, joint event) stays as a fixed tradition. Ramadan 2027 is expected to start around 8 Feb 2027, right after Boshontoboron.

Anything that depends on a decision no one has made yet is marked "Depends on: ...". No dates are invented beyond what the plan files give, except months.

---

## Presidents

**Winter outcome:** Run a lighter term on easier formats, keep Boshontoboron on track, and hand the club over cleanly at the end.

**Milestones**
- Jan: Confirm owners for each new low-coordination format (speaker meets, workshops, soccer, advocacy week, awareness series) at the first VP planning meeting.
- Jan: Review Winter availability (due 15 Jan) at that same meeting.
- Feb: Boshontoboron runs 6 Feb. Watch the Ramadan start (~8 Feb) before scheduling anything right after.
- Mar: Mid-term VP planning: look at what's working in the new formats, drop what isn't.
- Apr: Term wrap: recognition, handover notes for next year's exec. Depends on: the Spring/Summer exec transition timeline, not set in the files.

**Recurring rhythm:** Weekly 20-minute president sync, four fixed items. Monthly VP planning.

**Measure:** Tasks completed per exec.
