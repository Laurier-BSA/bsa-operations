## 6. Marketing: how to actually get attention

### Baseline first, on 25 Sep
Followers, average reach of the last ten posts, newsletter subscribers, general membership signups. Write them on the Marketing page. Without them, none of the targets mean anything at the wrap meeting.

### Six levers, in order of return

**1. Collab on everything.** An Instagram collab post appears on both accounts' feeds and pulls both audiences. Every event post should be co-authored with a co-host club, the venue, a sponsor, or the partner decorator. Target two collab posts a month. This is the single cheapest reach multiplier available and it costs nothing but an ask.

**2. Faces, not flyers.** Posters tell people an event exists. People make them care. "Faces of BSA" is one 15 second selfie video per exec or per team: name, program, role, favourite desi food, why they joined. Execs film their own on a phone, so it needs no photographer and no design time. Every person featured shares it, which is the point.

**3. The exec share rule.** Thirteen execs each sharing every post to their story within three hours reaches more people than the club account does. Announce it at the standup, treat it as part of the role, and check it in the weekly standup form. Free, and larger than anything else on this list.

**4. Three recurring series so posting never needs a decision.**
- Monday, culture: Bangla 101, a word or phrase, or a short explainer carousel.
- Wednesday, people or street: "guess the Bangladeshi dish", "say this in Bangla", reaction Reels filmed on campus in one afternoon and cut into four posts.
- Friday, club: event promo, recap, or a Faces post.

**5. The event content loop, every time.** Countdown sticker and link sticker in stories, a pre event Reel, live stories on the day, a photo dump within 48 hours tagging everyone who came, and a recap Reel within five days. Put a branded frame or backdrop at the flagship so attendees post their own photos and tag you. Turnout becomes next month's reach.

**6. Funnels at every table.** One QR at the booth and at the flagship door leading to a single link: Instagram, newsletter, membership form, next event signup. Right now a booth earns $5 and nothing else. A booth that adds 60 followers and 30 emails is worth running even at break even.

### Supporting moves
- Pin three posts: what BSA is, how to join, next event.
- Cross post every Reel to TikTok. Same file, two minutes, a completely different audience.
- Run one giveaway a term, tied to the flagship: follow, tag two Laurier friends, share to story, win two tickets or a piece of merch. More than one and the follows stop being real.
- Ask ten amplifier accounts to share the flagship: the SU, Laurier International, other Bangladeshi student associations in the region, local Bangladeshi restaurants, the sponsor.
- Newsletter twice: 13 Nov (flagship) and 18 Dec (term wrap plus winter teaser).
- Schedule in Meta Business Suite, free. The Notion Content Directory row holds the caption, the date, the asset and the numbers afterwards.

### Weekly rhythm
Monday 15 minutes: fill next week in the calendar view. Assets due to Creative three days before each post. Friday 5 minutes: log reach and follows on each posted row. Monthly at planning: look at the top three posts and do more of that, out loud, in front of the team.

### Guardrail
The Marketing VP approves and posts. No second approval layer. A club account dies of committee, not of a bad post.

Liaison means: first call for that team's blockers, reads their line on Team Progress weekly, attends their meeting when it matters. It also means you stop both attending everything, which is where most of your time goes.

### The weekly sync, 20 minutes, same four items

1. Team Progress: what is blocking, by team. Numbers only.
2. Money: owed to execs, allocated remaining. Numbers only.
3. Decisions needed this week, each with one name against it.
4. Who needs a nudge (Farhan does the nudging) and what needs fixing (Safdar does the fixing).

Log the decisions where you already log them, in the Presidents space. A decision without one owner is the thing that keeps stalling.

### Four things that would measurably improve your progress

1. **Send Farhan outputs, not systems.** He is a finance student who prefers Sheets. The reminder digest should send a five line presidents summary twice a week: overdue by team, open gates, owed to execs, allocated remaining. If he will read a read only Sheet export of Transactions, build that too. It is an hour of work and it ends the tooling argument.
2. **Freeze the schema until 8 Dec.** Say it out loud at the standup. It is the honest answer to "we are overcomplicating this", and it forces your remaining effort into adoption, which is where the value now is.
3. **Both of you run one initiative through the pipeline yourselves.** You will find the friction in week one instead of hearing about it in January. This is also the policy you already ratified.
4. **Make one thing visible every week.** A screenshot of the phase board at the standup, or the top three posts at planning. Systems get used when people see their own name moving on them.
