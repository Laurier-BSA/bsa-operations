# Photography

Small team, one job: every event leaves with usable stills and vertical video, in Drive, within 48 hours. Vertical video is the part that has been missing, and it is what Reels need.

## A. Workspace work

- [ ] `API` **P1 Shot list page** under Guides, referenced from the Photography handbook. Twelve standard shots: room wide before doors, queue or entry, crowd from the front, crowd from behind showing the room full, food table, decor detail, three candids of people talking, exec group photo, sponsor or partner signage, and three vertical clips of 5 to 10 seconds each for Reels.
