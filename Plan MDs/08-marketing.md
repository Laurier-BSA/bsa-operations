## C. Weekly rhythm

Monday, 15 minutes, fill next week in the calendar view and tell Creative what is needed. Posts Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Stories at least four days a week, daily in an event week. Friday, 5 minutes, log the numbers. The Marketing VP approves and posts, there is no second approval layer.
